How to tell them apart

parser: the tenant writes the day first and the shared parser reads the month first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Any channel, any day-first tenant. This is what #4471 describes.

hint: the tenant runs booking-v3, whose date field shows the hint MM/DD/YYYY. People type the month first, as the hint asks, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The date comes out flipped too, but the fix is the hint, not the parser. These come in through the form only, from booking-v3 tenants only. Intake tags them by the reporter’s browser, so the “tagged as” column on #4471 doesn’t match: go by tenant and channel, as causes.ts does.

one-day: the date moves back one day, not across months: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a meeting reaches Lisbon as Tuesday and Toronto as Monday. Nothing is flipped, so the parser fix won’t touch it. The desk had already noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but filed them under the parser by tenant.

Why they ended up together

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001), and all three problems arrive under the same subjects: “Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”. That page hasn’t been reviewed since June 2024. Until a new decision replaces it, check tenant and channel before merging a date report into a ticket.
