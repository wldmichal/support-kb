How to tell them apart

parser (fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales): the tenant writes the day first and the shared parser read the month first, so 7 April came out as 4 July. Any channel, any day-first tenant.

hint (won’t fix: booking-v3 retired): the tenant ran booking-v3, whose date field showed the hint MM/DD/YYYY. People typed the month first, as the hint asked, and the form read it the tenant’s way, day first. Form only, booking-v3 tenants only. Intake tagged them by the reporter’s browser, so the “tagged as” column on #4471 doesn’t match: go by tenant and channel, as causes.ts does.

one-day (open): the date moves back one day, not across months: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a meeting reaches Lisbon as Tuesday and Toronto as Monday. Nothing is flipped, so neither fix touches it. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) and counts them under the parser by tenant, which is why they closed with #4471.

Why they ended up together

Triage merges on subject line alone (decision 0001), and all three problems arrive under the same subjects: “Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”. That page hasn’t been reviewed since June 2024. Until a new decision replaces it, check tenant and channel before merging a date report into a ticket.
